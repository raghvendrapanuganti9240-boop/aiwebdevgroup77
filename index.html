Why fake news wins the race.

It is emotional, urgent and unsourced, so it spreads faster than a correction can. Watch the grid on the right settle as we separate claims from facts.

Four red flags to spot

1

Urgent, scary language

“Act now”, “last chance”, “before it is deleted”.
2

No named source

“Doctors say” or “my friend in the ministry” is not a source.
3

Asks you to forward it

Real notices don't depend on chain messages.
4

Too good or too shocking

Free money and instant cures deserve a second look.

No. It means no published fact-check was found yet. Wait for confirmation before sharing.
